Format for Discrepancy Letter to candidate during DV before Interview

Date: ______________


To,

[Candidate’s Full Name]

Address: [Candidate's Address]


Subject: Provisional Permission to Appear for Interview – Discrepancy in Eligibility


Dear Mr./Ms [Candidate’s Name],

With reference to your application for the post of ------------------- on ------------- in the ------------------------ dept, under the recruitment process initiated through the advertisement dated ------------------------------, certain discrepancies have been observed during the document verification stage prior to the interview.

Based on the documents submitted, your eligibility for the said post cannot be conclusively established as per the criteria prescribed in the official advertisement. The details of the discrepancies observed are as follows:

Sr. Nature of Discrepancy Document required to be submitted
1 Age mismatch between DOB proof and application. Aadhar Card

During the screening of your application, certain discrepancies have been identified in your submitted documents.

You are hereby required to upload the necessary valid and corrected documents addressing these discrepancies on or before [specified date].

Please note that your candidature will be considered for shortlisting for the interview only upon successful submission and verification of the required documents establishing your eligibility for the post.

Failure to submit the required documents within the stipulated timeframe will result in your candidature not being considered for further stages of the selection process.

The decision of the Bank in all matters pertaining to the selection process shall be final and binding.

Yours sincerely,

SS Recruitment
